# Choose tools for the next creative decision

Use the smallest combination that supplies the shot's required control and a practical review loop. Keep an existing working route unless a trial demonstrates a useful improvement. Availability, licensing and export behavior depend on the installed version; inspect the relevant primary documentation before adding a dependency. Capabilities were checked on 2026-11-01. Install only what the shot needs.

| Need | Candidate and role | Decision or limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frame-driven type, UI, media and sound assembly | [Remotion](https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion) and its [official skills](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/ai/skills) | A suitable primary renderer for React compositions. Keep action data, editorial timing and editable sound distinct. Technical guidance does not supply a film treatment. |
| HTML/CSS-based video authoring | [HyperFrames](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes) | An alternative seekable-media and video-rendering route. Compare one representative shot and a meaningful revision before migrating; do not assume a different renderer improves the choreography. |
| Believable authored product UI | `stageshow`, when available | Use its construction and rehearsal capabilities within the film's direction. Check its actual capture route; see [Authored UI](production.md#construct-authored-ui-for-the-shot). |
| DOM, SVG, layout or staggered animation | [Anime.js](https://animejs.com/documentation/timeline/) or [Motion](https://motion.dev/docs/animate) | Useful local motion tools when their controls fit the shot. Drive them from the authoritative timeline; verify seeking and callbacks instead of layering independent clocks. |
| Direct human control over a comparison | [Tweakpane](https://github.com/cocopon/tweakpane) or native editor controls | Expose meaningful parameters and save approved presets. See [Creative comparisons](review.md#make-creative-comparisons-easy-to-direct). |
| Controlled physical product imagery | Blender/Cycles, optionally through [MCP for Blender](https://github.com/ahujasid/mcp-for-blender) | A connector can shorten scene inspection and adjustment; it does not improve rendering quality by itself. Verify the connection, preserve the editable scene and use [3D review](controlled-3d.md). |
| Browser-native 3D and interactive look development | [Three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js), optionally [postprocessing](https://github.com/pmndrs/postprocessing) or [Three GPU Pathtracer](https://github.com/gkjohnson/three-gpu-pathtracer) | Choose according to required appearance and runtime. Progressive path tracing needs a defined sample budget per frame and restarts accumulation after scene changes. Verify color and temporal stability before treating a preview as a final render. |
| Physical interaction or particles that carry the idea | [Jolt Physics](https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics) or [Three.quarks](https://github.com/Alchemist0823/three.quarks) | Add simulation only for a shot that needs it. Control timestep and randomness; bake or cache the approved motion. A real-time simulation is not necessarily seekable. |
| Repeatable generated asset candidates | [ComfyUI](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI) | Preserve workflow and model provenance; verify product identity and continuity throughout the output. A repeatable graph does not guarantee a repeatable or editable image. |
| Music landmarks, voice timing or scratch narration | [librosa](https://github.com/librosa/librosa), [WhisperX](https://github.com/m-bain/whisperX), [CosyVoice](https://github.com/QwenAudio/CosyVoice) | Respectively analyze music, align speech and synthesize voice. The user and agent choose music, direct performance and review through listening; see [Music and sound](music-and-sound.md). |
| Real captures, source preparation and delivery | [OBS](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio), [LosslessCut](https://github.com/mifi/lossless-cut), [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.html) | Capture planned takes, prepare media, then inspect and encode explicitly. Stream-copy trimming may be keyframe-limited; use the edit's timeline for exact final boundaries. |
| Precise mathematical explanation or a companion vector animation | [Manim](https://docs.manim.community/en/stable/) or [Lottie-web](https://github.com/airbnb/lottie-web) | Manim can author a necessary diagram; Lottie-web plays exported animation. Use only when the deliverable benefits from that specific capability. |

Judge a proposed tool on the actual output and the next revision it must support. Keep source fonts, assets, frame cadence and color interpretation consistent during a comparison. Record separate results for construction, seeking, playback, export and final-image quality wherever the trial covers them.
